COMPETITIVE EXAMS

Agriculture + General Studies reference

The supplied Udhaividhai exam guide consolidates Agriculture and civil-services pathways. It explicitly advises checking the latest official notification for current eligibility, pattern and dates.

01

UPSC Civil Services

Prelims → Mains → Personality Test. The supplied guide covers GS Prelims, CSAT and Agriculture Optional topics.

02

Indian Forest Service

The supplied guide includes IFoS stages and identifies Agriculture among the optional-subject pathways in its reference.

03

TNPSC Agricultural Officer

The supplied guide lists Agriculture and General Studies papers and a 10-unit Agriculture syllabus framework.

04

TNPSC Group I

Prelims and descriptive Mains structure are covered, including General Studies and Tamil Nadu-focused areas.

05

TNPSC ACF

The supplied guide includes Prelims, Mains and an optional-subject reference that includes Agriculture and related sciences.

06

Agriculture Core

Common preparation themes include crop production, soil and water, crop improvement, seed science, horticulture, protection, economics and extension.

Verification note: This website is a structured reference based on the supplied materials, not an official examination notification. Verify the latest notification, eligibility, age, reservation, syllabus, paper pattern, optional restrictions and dates with the relevant authority.
